# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no frameworks, no jQuery (user's choice). Performance is a hard requirement: the reference site (adhamdannaway.com) makes 31 requests and takes ~4 s to load; this one must be near-instant.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and potential clients evaluating a person who works across marketing and software development. They skim; they decide in seconds whether this person can bridge both worlds.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Fernando (surname and exact title still to confirm). It shows two professional sides, Marketing and Development, as one person. Success: a visitor understands the dual profile in the first viewport and contacts him.

## Positioning

One person who speaks both languages: campaign strategy and the code that ships it. The split-face hero (marketing | development) is the signature idea, borrowed structurally from adhamdannaway.com (designer | coder).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v1 sections: hero with Marketing | Development split, work/cases grid, about, contact.
- No blog in v1.
- Language: Spanish by default (to confirm).
- Open: real project cases, final title/headline, contact links. Placeholders must be labelled as examples.

## Evidence on Hand

- `foto.jpg` (3584x4772, 7.5 MB): frontal portrait, white background, navy t-shirt. Provided as a test photo; must be optimised and never served at original size.
- No real case studies, clients, metrics or testimonials supplied yet. Do not fabricate them as real.

## Product Principles

1. The two halves are equal: neither marketing nor development is the "main" job.
2. Lightness is part of the message: a developer's site that loads instantly.
3. Show the split, don't explain it.
4. No invented proof.
